# The Thermal Lens Revisited: Emergence of Microstructure, Scarcity-Induced Curvature, and Hot-Spot Regularization

**R. V. Adams, K. A. Lurie, W. C. Sanguinet**

Talk given at the RTG conference, University of Utah, Salt Lake City, 17 October 2026.
Dedicated to Andrej Cherkaev and Graeme Milton.

**Abstract.** We celebrate forty years of progress on this foundational problem and motivate the inevitable emergence
of microstructure with new insights. We then present what are, to our knowledge, the first numerical results for the
volume-fraction-constrained problem. These results also address an engineering concern: hot spots, both throughout
the domain and across the window itself.
